Netpulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

In short: Netpulse does not collect, store or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The app only sends test traffic to a public speed-test server so it can measure your connection.

This policy explains how the Netpulse mobile app (“the app”), published by Rosa Corp (“we”, “us”), handles information. Netpulse shows live ping, download and upload speed for your current internet connection.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not operate a server that receives data from the app, and we do not require you to create an account or sign in.

2. Information stored on your device

The app lets you type two optional settings: a ping limit (in milliseconds) and a name that the voice alert speaks. These values are kept only in the app’s memory while it is open. They are not saved to storage, not sent anywhere, and are erased when you close the app. Ping readings and speed results are also shown only on your screen and are not stored or transmitted to us.

3. Network requests to third parties

To measure your connection, the app sends ordinary network requests to Cloudflare’s public speed-test service (speed.cloudflare.com). These requests contain only test data (empty or filler bytes), not your name or any personal content. As with any internet request, Cloudflare’s servers receive your device’s IP address and request timing, and Cloudflare handles that under its own privacy policy, available at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. We do not receive this information.

The speed test downloads and uploads data and can use several megabytes of mobile data. Please run it on Wi‑Fi if you have a limited data plan.

4. Voice alerts

The optional voice alert uses the text-to-speech feature built into your phone to say “ping is dropping”, together with the name you typed. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone. How the speech voice is produced depends on your device’s speech engine and its settings.

5. Permissions

The app needs internet access to run its tests. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

6. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or share your data, because we do not have it.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Data retention and your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retain, export or delete. Closing the app clears everything it held. If you have a question about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, you can contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
